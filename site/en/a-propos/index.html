About

Offensive research · OSINT · EASM · Attack Path Analysis · IT/OT

Under the name lxrider, I conduct independent offensive cybersecurity research, focusing primarily on industrial and cyber-physical environments.

My background combines Linux engineering, networking and IT/OT cybersecurity with training in private investigation.

Investigating adversarial possibilities

My approach involves targeted offensive intelligence that informs security work before Red Team operations.

Starting from an organization's critical functions, I investigate its exposures, adversarial capabilities and potential attack paths.

I am particularly interested in how an adversary might prepare an operation, create favorable conditions or exploit opportunities underestimated by defenders.

I test hypotheses against the evidence, technical constraints and the defender's ability to respond.

From systems to consequences

My investigations connect access paths, privileges and trust boundaries to potential operational consequences.

They include research into commercial refrigeration cybersecurity. A study of building management and control systems (BMS) is in preparation.

I also develop my hands-on offensive skills through labs and practical exercises.

A guiding principle: distinguish established facts, hypotheses and what remains unknown.

Independent analysis of systems and their access paths.
